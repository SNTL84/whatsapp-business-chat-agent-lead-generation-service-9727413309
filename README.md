**Q2. Is this a chatbot with AI or rule-based?**  
A. Express package = high-converting rule-based + menu-driven agent (perfect for most SMBs). Advanced AI/LLM agentic flows available as upgrade.

**Q3. Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account?**  
A. No. We configure it on the official WhatsApp Business App. API version available for higher volume later.

**Q4. How long does Express Setup take?**  
A. Usually same day or next business day after you share business details, menu items, and FAQs.

**Q5. Can it take orders / payments?**  
A. Yes — catalog, order forms, and payment links (UPI / Razorpay etc.).

**Q6. Will it work 24/7?**  
A. Yes. Auto-replies and menu work offline. You only get notified for high-intent leads or “Talk to human”.

**Q7. Can I edit the agent later myself?**  
A. Yes. We train you on updating messages, adding options, and managing labels.

**Q8. Is there any monthly fee?**  
A. ₹999 is one-time. No mandatory monthly fee from our side.

**Q9. How does the profile-exchange verification work?**

**Q10. Can the agent handle long-term / old contacts?**  
A. Yes. It can reactivate with branded intro, answer service questions, share location/hours, and book meetings (see Live Demo 2).

**Q11. What information do you need from me to start?**
